CustomerWho exactly is it for?Visitors to that yard sale, that day

Materials and inventoryWhat do you need, and how much?Lemons, sugar, water, ice, cups, a jug, a table
LogisticsHow do things get where they need to be?Buying supplies, keeping ice cold, refilling in time
OperationsWhat is the daily routine?Make, pour, serve, clean, restock
PricingWhat will you charge, and how?Per cup? A bigger cup for more?
Profit marginWhat is left after costs?Price of a cup minus what is inside it
MarketingHow do people find out?A sign, a good spot, a friendly call
Sales strategyHow do you turn interest into a yes?Offer it when people are hot and waiting
