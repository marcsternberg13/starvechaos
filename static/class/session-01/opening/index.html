What the researchers found

Curiosity is
a hunger.

People gambled on a small electric shock to learn how a magic trick worked, much as hungry people gambled to get food. Saying yes went with more activity in the same reward area of the brain, the striatum, in both cases.

The stance

Starve
chaos.

Do not feed it guesses, vague plans, hidden disagreements and numbers you have not checked. Feed your business evidence, clear choices and small bets you can survive.
