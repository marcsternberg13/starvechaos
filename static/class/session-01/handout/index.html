For your final project

Start your business plan today.

Your final presentation in Week 16 must answer all 19 rows for your own business. Begin with these three questions.

  1. Where are your future customers already gathered? Name the place and the time of day.
  2. Which row of the table will be hardest for your business, and why?
  3. What would one small, cheap test of your idea look like, with an answer on the same day?
