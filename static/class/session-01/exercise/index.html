Chaos card · 10 minutes

Chaos card.

Your teacher hands your group a card. It changes the day at your stand. You have ten minutes to respond, using the 19 parts of a business.

Why chaos cards

Chaos is normal.

Suppliers stop, rules change, customers ask for half the price. The skill is not to avoid chaos. It is to respond to it.
