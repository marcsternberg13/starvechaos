The Starve Chaos Curriculum
Hunger, Curiosity & Chaos

A family in your city or town has a car, a big kitchen, and a cousin who runs a stall where many people pass each day. They have no new money to spend. What business could they start this week? Picture the family, and work from imagination. You may add family details that fit the case.

Before Session 08
