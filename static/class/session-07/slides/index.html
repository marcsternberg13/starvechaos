Rule 30

Start with the money you have, not the money you want.

What you have

Start with what you already have: savings, skills and the people you know.

What you can lose

Risk only what you can afford to lose (Sarasvathy, 2001).

Waiting costs

Waiting for the money you want can mean you never start.

Rule 31

Do things by hand before you build the machine.

One at a time

Early on, do the work yourself, one customer at a time.

Things that don’t scale

Paul Graham (2013) calls this doing things that don’t scale.

Slow, but it teaches

You learn what matters before you pay to automate it. Work by hand tells you what to build.

Rule 24

Cut every cost the customer never sees.

Save where they don’t look

Save on what buyers never see, and never on what they value.

One test

Test every cut with one question: will the customer notice?

Hidden is not always hidden

Cutting safety, staff pay or quality checks saves money today and costs trust later.

Rule 25

Look at what you already own before you buy.

Unused assets

Most ventures sit on unused space, machines, skills, materials and relationships.

Faster and cheaper

Using them for something new is often faster and cheaper than buying anything new.

List first
