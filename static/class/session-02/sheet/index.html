Read each case. Write F for failure or C for chaos. For each chaos case, write which of the four causes it is.

CaseWhat happenedF or CCause of chaos
AA tutor gives ten families a free first lesson to see if they will book a second. Two book. She writes down which families booked, and why.
BA tailor is busier every month. Every month there is less cash in the drawer. Nobody knows why.
CThree friends run a delivery service. Each one thinks another one orders the boxes. One morning there are no boxes.
DA bakery rents a stand at a weekend fair, with a limit of 500 units of money set in advance. Sales cover half. The owner learns that fair visitors want snacks, not whole loaves.
ETwo partners run a juice stand. One wants to grow fast. The other wants to stay small. Neither says so. They argue about every purchase.
FA clothing seller opens an online shop. Orders come in, but nobody planned how returns work. Angry customers leave bad reviews.
GA car wash offers a monthly plan to 20 customers for one month. Only 5 renew. The owner asks why and learns they wanted it only in summer.
HA café tries opening at 6 a.m. for two weeks. Before it starts, the owner writes: stop if fewer than ten customers come each morning. Four come. The café stops.
