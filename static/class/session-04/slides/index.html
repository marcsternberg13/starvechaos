Rule 6

Being better is not the same as being different.

Operational effectiveness

Doing the same activities better than rivals. Necessary, but rivals can copy it.

Strategic position

Doing different activities, or the same ones in a different way, so you are not compared on price alone.

Key words

Goal and strategy.

WORDWHAT IT MEANS
GoalWhere you want to be, such as “double sales in two years.”
StrategyA small set of choices about who you serve, what you offer and why customers pick you. It tells people what to do without asking the boss.

Source: Sternberg (2026), Section 01 key terms

25 / 53

Rule 1

A goal is a wish; strategy is a few choices.

A few core choices

A strategy turns the goal into a small set of core choices that let each person decide alone (Van den Steen, 2017).

Three parts

A clear strategy statement names the objective, the scope and the advantage (Collis & Rukstad, 2008).

The copy test

Could a rival copy the choice word for word? If one could, it is not yet a choice.

Key words

Two kinds of door.

DOORWHAT IT MEANS
Two-way doorA decision you can reverse. Decide these fast.
One-way doorA decision you cannot reverse, such as a long lease or a large loan. Slow down for these.

Rule 11

Decide with most of the facts, not all of them.

Waiting is a decision

Waiting for certainty is also a decision, and usually a costly one.

About 70%

Bezos argues that most decisions should be made with about 70% of the information you wish you had (Bezos, 2017).

Then fix it

Owners decide once they know enough, explain why, and fix the choice if it proves wrong.
