Rule 15

Watch what people actually use, not what experts predict.

Behavior, not forecasts

What people really do tells you more than what experts forecast.

What they change

Study what customers install, fix, hack and change without being asked. Their behavior shows needs no survey will find.

Lead users

Customers who feel a need before the rest of the market, and often build their own fix (von Hippel, 1986).

Rule 17

When the rules change, a new door opens.

New rules, new doors

New platforms, laws and reward systems create new openings.

Move early, learn first

While the rules are still being written, the people who move early can learn them first and help shape them.

Platform risk

The same change that opens a door can close it. If you depend on one platform or one law, plan for the next change.

Rule 13

List the rules everyone follows, then break one.

Write them down

Every industry runs on rules that nobody questions. Write them down.

What if one is false?

The rule everyone accepts is often where customers are least happy.

Not every rule is waste

Some rules exist for safety, law or trust. Break the ones that only protect habit.
