Task 2 · Habit or safety? 5 minutes

H · Habit
Done this way because it always was. You may break it.
S · Safety
Protects people from harm. Keep it.
L · Law
Breaking it is illegal. Keep it.
T · Trust

Session 05 · Opportunity · Group work

Your team.

The Starve Chaos Curriculum
Hunger, Curiosity & Chaos

Problem Map box 3 Current options · second draft · 30 minutes

Start from your first draft in Session 4 and your two conversations from homework.

Workarounds: what people put together themselves to cope with your problem.

What people avoid: what they skip, delay or go without because no option fits.

The rule everyone follows: list the rules every current option follows. Mark each H, S, L or T. Circle one H rule your idea could break.

What if …?

Chaos log: what did your team not know today? A failure you understand, or chaos you do not? What will you do?

For your final project

Which rule will your idea break?

Your final presentation must show the opportunity: the need, the buyer who pays, and your way to meet it. Start now: write the rule everyone in your industry follows, and the one your idea breaks.
