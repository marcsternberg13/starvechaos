Rule 19

Start with someone’s real problem, not a shiny tool.

A real pain

Good openings come from a pain people feel or a limit they keep hitting.

A shiny tool

A new technology with no clear problem is a tool looking for a use. It usually finds no buyer.

Bottleneck

The one step that slows everything else. Problems often hide there.

Rule 14

The customer everyone ignores is often the opening.

Look for the left-out

Look for people an industry overlooks or leaves out.

An open market

If a group spends money but no one serves it well, that group is an open market.

Check they can pay

Sometimes a group is ignored because serving it costs more than it earns.
