Task 2 · Find the empty row 5 minutes

Look across each row. Circle any row where all three options say No. That row is the empty space. If no row is all No, circle the row with the most No marks.

Your team.

Problem Map box 2 Pain · 20 minutes

Start from box 3, second draft, and the workarounds you watched for homework. Write the pain the way your customer would say it.

What hurts: the pain, in your customer’s own words.

How often: every day, every week, once a year?

How much: what it costs them in time, money or worry.

The bottleneck: the one step where it hurts most.

The gap: map your current options from box 3 against the pain. Every option …, but none …

Chaos card 10 minutes

1. What changed for your customer, your pain or your gap? 2. Which rule helps? 3. What will your team do this week? 4. Chaos log: a failure you understand, or chaos you do not?

For your final project

What gap will your idea fill?

Your final presentation must show the gap: what every current option misses, and the customer standing in it. Start now: write one sentence. Every option …, but none ….

Before Session 07

  1. Ask two people who have your team’s problem: what does every option they tried miss? Write down their words.
  2. List three things you have that you have never used for a business: a skill, a tool, a space or a person you know.
  3. Map keeper: keep box 2 and the gap sentence. Chaos-log keeper: keep today’s chaos-card entry in the log.
