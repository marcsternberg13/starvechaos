3 · Test your wall: three customers walk in

Who walks inIn or out?
1A customer wants a copy of a famous brand’s jacket, label and all. Pays double.
2A customer from a group you left out. Needs one small job today. Pays on time.
3A customer from your group wants a rush job in two days. Your tailors are full.

Wall or weight? If a higher price would change your answer, it was a weight. If nothing would change it, it is a wall.
