Chaos cards.

The Starve Chaos Curriculum
Hunger, Curiosity & Chaos

Chaos card · 1

A big buyer outside your wall offers to pay twice your price.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 2

A friend says your industry is boring and your team should choose another one.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 3

You find out your customer group is about 30 people, not 300.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 4

A customer inside your wall asks for the one thing your wall says you will not do.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 5

Your industry has many weak rivals, and almost nobody makes money in it.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 6

Most of your customers turn out to live in a different part of the city than you thought.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 7

A large company asks your team to serve all its staff, but only at the cheapest price.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 8

Your best customers can only be reached on weekends, when every member of your team is busy.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Source: Sternberg (2026), Rules 2 and 12; Part 5 (cards written for class). Sternberg, M. (2026). Hunger, curiosity & chaos: 101 ways entrepreneurs think. Son of a Botanist Productions.
