A new phone repair stall in your city or town. The owner has 3,000 units of money to start. Not one unit more: no loan, no investor. The owner’s wish list adds up to 6,000.

The wish list Tasks 1 and 2 · 17 minutes

Task 1: mark each line F (fixed: paid whether you sell or not, such as rent) or V (variable: rises with each sale, such as materials). Task 2: keep it, cut it, or make it cheaper, and write the new number. For every cut, ask: will the customer notice? Write Y or N. The new total must be 3,000 or less.
