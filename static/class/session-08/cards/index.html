Chaos cards.

The Starve Chaos Curriculum
Hunger, Curiosity & Chaos

Chaos card · 1

A big company says it will start serving your small customers next year.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 2

Your budget is cut in half. You can keep only one thing that costs real money.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 3

Your team has no shop. A rival with a shop laughs at you.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 4

Your first test sale works, but after costs, each sale loses money.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 5

The customers in the season or place you chose cannot pay until the end of the month.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 6

A seller offers free advertising for one month if you sign up to pay for the next six.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 7

A customer says she will not trust a business run by students.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Chaos card · 8

The one thing you spent big on breaks in the first week.

You have ten minutes. Which rule helps? What will you do this week?

Source: Sternberg (2026), Rules 11, 22, 32 and 33; Part 5 (cards written for class). Sternberg, M. (2026). Hunger, curiosity & chaos: 101 ways entrepreneurs think. Son of a Botanist Productions.
