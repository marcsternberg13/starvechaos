Rule 32

Go where the big players won’t.

What they skip

Large firms often skip small places, small customers and awkward seasons.

Why it works

Those markets are often cheaper to serve and easier to win.

The check

Start where the big firms are not looking. Check that the customers there can still pay you.

Rule 33

Turn a weakness into an advantage.

List

List what you lack.

Ask

Then ask how that gap could help you. Lacking something rivals have can push you to a better design.

Up to a point

Limits help creativity up to a point; too many limits, or none, can hurt (Acar et al., 2019).
